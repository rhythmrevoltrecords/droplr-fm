## Rules

- **One a day, by hand.** At this size the first hour of comments is the whole value, and
  a scheduler hands that hour to nobody.
- **Never cross-post the droplr caption to a music page.** Different audience, different job.
- **Say I, not we** on droplr. The label page can say we.
- **Three or four hashtags** a real person would follow. No walls.
- Never say platform, hub, solution, ecosystem, all-in-one, game-changer, revolutionise.
- Nothing from the roadmap. Only what's live.
